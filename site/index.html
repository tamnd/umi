umi

An internet scale web crawler, in Rust.

umi fetches the open web continuously and publishes everything it collects as open data under open-index on Hugging Face. The local disk is a cache. Once a batch of pages is published and verified, the local copy is deleted.

It is the acquisition layer for an open index. Crawling is the part of a search engine that nobody can borrow, so it is the part that has to exist first.
