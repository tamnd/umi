Rules for the slipway

The rules are posted at the top of the ramp on a board that has been there since before anybody currently using the slipway was born, and they are reproduced here in the order they appear on it.

  1. Launch on the flood, recover on the ebb.
    • If you must recover on the flood, wait for someone else to be present.
    • The winch is not a substitute for the tide.
      1. The winch cable is inspected in March.
      2. The winch cable has failed twice.
  2. Trailers off the ramp within ten minutes.

  3. Nobody launches alone after dark.

    This one is not negotiable and the reason is in the minutes for 1987.

The board is repainted every five years and the rules have not changed once.

  • Except for the ten minutes, which used to be twenty.
