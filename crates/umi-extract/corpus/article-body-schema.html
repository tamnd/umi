The gauge is back

By a secretary

The tide gauge on the north wall has been repaired and is reading correctly for the first time since February, when a piece of driftwood the size of a door took out the float housing during the storm.

The replacement was paid for out of the maintenance fund, which means the resurfacing of the slipway moves to next year, which surprised nobody at the meeting where it was announced.
